ArtSpeak

Abstract

ArtSpeak is a standalone mobile AR application that lets museum visitors ask questions of art portraits and receive lifelike talking-head responses. It retrieves pre-generated answers to reduce latency and energy use while keeping the experience responsive and engaging.

Open LocalMate project page Interspeech 2026 · Accepted!

LocalMate

Abstract

LocalMate is an end-to-end, accent-adaptive conversational agent for public airport information. It combines confidence-aware accent estimation, grounded FAQ retrieval, and accent-conditioned speech, using a neutral fallback whenever accent evidence is uncertain.
