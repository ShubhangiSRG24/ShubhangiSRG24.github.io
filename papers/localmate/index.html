When Machines Speak Like Local Peers: Improving Conversational Experiences with Accent-Adaptive Voice Agents

Accepted at Interspeech 2026

PDF Overview System Availability

Abstract

LocalMate is an end-to-end accent-adaptive conversational agent for public airport information. It estimates a speaker’s accent with calibrated confidence, retrieves a grounded answer from a validated FAQ set, and returns accent-conditioned speech with synchronized talking-face video.

The system uses a confidence-gated fallback instead of guessing when accent evidence is weak. The study compares baseline U.S. speech, correct adaptation, incorrect adaptation, and neutral fallback to examine how adaptation quality affects confidence, trust, and interaction friction.

System

Accent estimation

Use WavLM-Base-Plus with layer-weighted statistics pooling to estimate accent and confidence.

Grounded retrieval

Transcribe the utterance and retrieve the closest answer from a validated airport FAQ set.

Safe realization

Generate accent-conditioned speech and talking-face video, with neutral speech when confidence is low.

Study framing

Baseline voice

Compare against a fixed U.S. English output to measure the value of adaptation itself.

Correct adaptation

Test whether matching accent-conditioned synthesis improves confidence, trust, and perceived ease.

Fallback behavior

Use a neutral response path when accent evidence is weak rather than risking an incorrect adaptation.
