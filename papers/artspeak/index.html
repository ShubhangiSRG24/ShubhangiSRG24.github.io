ArtSpeak: An Interactive AR Application for Lifelike Speaking with Art Portraits

Full paper, IEEE International Symposium on Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR), 2025

Shubhangi S. R. Garnaik · Aruna Balasubramanian · Niranjan Balasubramanian · Jihoon Ryoo

PDF Publication record Demo

Abstract

Museum visits often lack personalized and interactive experiences. ArtSpeak is a standalone mobile AR application that lets visitors point a camera at an artwork, ask questions, and receive lifelike talking-head responses from the portrait. To avoid the cost of generating video at runtime, the system prepares responses for curated frequently asked questions and retrieves the closest match using GPT-based embeddings and cosine similarity.

This retrieval-based approach achieves more than 30× lower latency and approximately 81% lower energy use than live video generation. In the user study, 85% of participants rated responses as relevant and 90% reported smooth video playback.

System

Offline setup

Generate FAQ pairs and embeddings, synthesize audio, and pre-render portrait videos with SADTALKER.

Runtime retrieval

Transcribe the visitor’s question, retrieve the closest FAQ, and play the matched response immediately.

Graceful fallback

Use an introductory response and a generated fallback when the curated FAQ set has no suitable match.
